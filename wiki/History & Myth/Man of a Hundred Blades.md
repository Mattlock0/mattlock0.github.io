The **Man of a Hundred Blades** is a mythological hero of old who was said to have defeated an entire army of one hundred Rootless men by himself, using only his [[Solid Vein]] as defense. The place of his death, where he supposedly planted blade-first the weapon of every man he killed, is called the [[Field of a Hundred Blades]].
