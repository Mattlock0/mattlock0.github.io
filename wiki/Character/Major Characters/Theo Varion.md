**Theo Varion** is one of the [[Major Characters]] in [[Northern Hearth]].
## 18 | Rakuken, East Tiereth

A tall (6’1), lithe young man with a sharp-cut jaw and dark, red hair. His eyes are orange, like his father, and he carries the [[Tattoos of Cultivation]] from his mother. His hands are covered in callouses, his arms often in bruises, and a large burn streaks up the shoulder of his right arm.
## Role in the Story
Brother and rival of [[Sai Varion|Sai]] until Sai leaves Rakuken, and Theo is forced to take the responsibilities he left behind. Sai’s primary oppressor in his personal history, but is left in the past as Sai journeys to [[Adraine]] and realizes the similarities between his conflict with Theo and this.
