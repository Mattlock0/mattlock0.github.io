**Avis Freya** is one of the [[Major Characters]] in [[Northern Hearth]].
## 18 | Nomad, Carlen

A muscular young woman of broad stature when she stands straight (5’9) with amethyst-colored eyes and brown stringy hair, often cut short. A black mask covers the lower half of her face made of a dark, jagged metal that swirls like the broiling sea. She has a sharp jaw, sharper nose, and rectangular eyebrows with ends that come to points. All her features are thin: sharp eyes, eyebrows, and lips.
## Role in the Story
Orphan and main character of [[Warmaker]]. Hunts [[valkrin]] with a vengeance for being the tool through which her people, the [[Caelish]], are suppressed. Has a particular hatred for the Knights; long ago stole one of their greatest weapons, and is now on the run.
