**Sai Varion** is one of the [[Major Characters]] in [[Northern Hearth]].
## 19 | Rakuken, East Tiereth

A young man of medium height (5’8) with a shock of straight copper-colored hair and green almond eyes. He has a slight slouch from working on wood projects, and his fingers are thin and dexterous. Has some muscle—mostly due to physical labor around his town—yet his shoulders are often hunched and his head kept low.
## Role in the Story
The main character of [[Peacemaker]] and the son of The Final Hero. Wields the [[Altreoch|Altreoch]] to Sever [[Fate]] and step into Freed Worlds. Leaves his home in Rakuken, East Tiereth to enter into the generations-old conflict between the [[Caelish]] and the [[Knights]] and resolve once and for all the feud that has plagued [[Adraine]] for years.
