The **Undrough** are a [[Culture]] in [[Northern Hearth]] that reside mostly in [[The Arden Alliance]]. Most Undrough are [[Tebrene]], though some are [[Madrian]].

The undrough are [[Caelish]] and [[Entic]] people who have given their freedom over to [[The Arden Alliance]] and the [[Knights]] and have not yet journeyed to [[Lothrame]] to establish their House.

They are made up of powerful, dense people groups with rich history and lives extending into the far past, but rather than stand on those histories and fight for freedom, they bow their heads, grumble, and work for the only ones who can protect them.



When the Caelish were given the name undrough by the Arden it was deeply offensive to them. In their language, undrough means “low (un) + ground (dr) + persist (ou) + through (gh)”, or something like “bottom-feeders” to us. [[Avis Freya|Avis]], like many Caelish, call those who have given into the subjugation of the Knights “undrough”. They use it in their own circles as a shameful name.

Most Undrough have remnants of the nomadic Caelish culture from which they came.
