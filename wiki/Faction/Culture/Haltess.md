The **Haltess** are a [[Culture]] in [[Northern Hearth]] which reside mostly in [[Aristocracy of Perholt]]. Most Haltess are [[Hadrill]].
## Puritism
The Haltess are purists who believe—not so different from the [[Arden]]—that purity of blood is important. They don’t care as much how long your family has been around, though, they care where you came from. This is often proved by the Tattoos of Cultivation, a racial feature of [[Hadrill]]. In Haltess culture, it is not considered immodest to reveal ones’ back unless it is bare.


They deport non-Haltess so they can’t tempt their people, and forbid their people from marrying outsiders. It’s the strongest towards the center of Perholt where the ruins of Durant lay, now known as the Snake’s Head.


Most Haltess are believers of [[Barosha]], those who hold Ancients as sacred messengers from [[The Architect]]. Those that aren’t [[Barosha]] are [[Tree Folk]], living so close to the Axis Tree.


Their primary cultural focus is intention. They don't have words for “sorry”, because if you meant to do it there should be no apology. They have much looser laws, and those they do have are the barest to keep peace. If someone believes they were treated unjustly, they can debate their attacker in court, and a decision will be made for that situation and that situation alone.
## Linguistics
Their names are inspired by Jewish and Greek.
