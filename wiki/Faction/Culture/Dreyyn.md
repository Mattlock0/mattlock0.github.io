The **Dreyyn** are a [[Culture]] in [[Northern Hearth]] which reside mostly in [[Democracy of Dreyynir]]. Most Dreyyn are [[Sennti]]. 

They believe that all land belongs to all people. Together, they are all the Nir. So, they have Nir names for every country, like Eleddnir (Eldenguard) and Perllnir (Perholt).

They do not like the Ancients, because they “broke” how the Nir should live. Humans own the earth; *God* owns the Tree.
## Vocations
In Dreyynir, men and women are all expected to follow a Vocation. This can be anything, so long as it requires work, can be mastered, and aids society in some way. Usually this is something like Fighter, or Leader, but it can also be traits like Honor or Meek.

Generally, Dreyyn call each other (and outsiders) by their Vocation, or a Vocation they believe them to have. They call them -so and -sa for male and female, respectively. A name might be Temper-so or Builder-sa. They use these as compliments/insults. They will observe a person until a trait stands out, at which point they call them that.

Many look down on those who do not embody any Vocation. Early on they teach their children to pursue a Vocation with all their hearts. If everyone pursues the Vocation they have been given, all needs will be satisfied.

Once a Dreyyn has pursued a [[Vocation]] long enough to be considered a master, their suffix changes to -to and -ra (m/f). This is a great honor, even to a Dreyyn.
## Linguistics
All the places and people in Dreyynir have a pair of letters somewhere in their name. The pair of letters designate a splitting point for the name, where each half means something different. They draw this tradition from Riddem, and they believe there was power between the splitting of Rid and dem of her name. Otherwise, their names are inspired by Welsh, Scottish, and Norse.
