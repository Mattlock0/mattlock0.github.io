The **Arte** are a [[Culture]] in [[Northern Hearth]] which reside mostly in [[The Fallen Kingdom of Artem]]. Most Arte are [[Hadrill]].

By this point in history, Arte are broken into many small, tribal groups eager to grow in power where they can, but rarely able to handle the vast campaigns they used to take on Tiereth soil.
