The **Arden** are a [[Culture]] in [[Northern Hearth]] that reside mostly in [[The Arden Alliance]]. Most Arden are [[Hadrill]], and the rest are [[Tebrene]] and [[Madrian]].



The Arden have advanced beyond the basic need for survival, as well as the huddled groups of tent-dwellers in their past. They lead the world (for the time being) in technology, law, and death count. Eldenguard is a Class-3 society (as dictated by the Abstraction Levels) and as such, are very exploitative as a whole.

They have a cultural focus on men, where only the firstborn boys are important. Men are the only ones who can lead a House, and women cannot join any order of the [[Knights]]. Boys often learn by accompanying their fathers to House meetings and trade negotiations. As of late, women have grown what little power they have and are not strictly relegated to home work, but it’s still very difficult for them to get a job in politics, and impossible in the military.

Rather than execute rebel [[Undrough]], the Arden take them, break them, and make them slaves. It's common to see rows of chained undrough being led on the road.


While not as evil as Voskarn, the Arden people are still quite militaristic, having warred on most of their continent until they were in control.

The Arden have an understanding of the Spiritual Plane that not many races do: they believe every person, concept, and some places, are invested with spiritual energy that gives them power over the physical world. (This transfers to their fear of the ocean: it has few “physical” landmarks and therefore is devoid of spiritual energy.)

They, along with the Haltess and some Dreyyn, are the first to pursue painting as an art medium. They also created many stringed instruments, along with some Vein-powered ones. They also have the Architeria, or the “Grand Library”. They’ve also taken some of the sports the Dreyyn created, and have made some of their own. They’re slow to start, though.
## Linguistics
Inspired by Rome and Germany.

Their primary spoken language (and the one they forced over the Arden continent) is Elian. It used to be Arden, but when the Alliance was formed they wanted to make it sound more…global, and changed the name. Some textbooks still reference the old name.

Arden surnames usually combine two separate syllables, like Fulgard or Belmire, and connect them with two consonants.
