The **Caelish** are a [[Culture]] in [[Northern Hearth]] which reside mostly in [[Carlen]]. Most Caelish are [[Tebrene]].

Caelish are pale white because they don't have the time or money to spend bathing in the sun. Eldenguard had access to Fate and pursued Dominion early, so it allowed them to advance quicker than the Caelish and now they have tanner skin *and* control of their continent.




They travel often and together; the Caelish, as [[Tebrene]], are imbued with the Ideal of Rally, and their comradery with one another shows. Unfortunately, this made them easy targets for the [[Arden]] to strike with their fire and steel. The only few standing Caelish cities are densely-packed and serve as rest points for travelers.

They share many Arden’s fear of the sea, but it’s not as strong in the Rim. Much of the fear was instilled in them from the Arden; before, they were quite the seafaring race, with what little technology they had to do it with. Much of their “fleet” was burned or given up to the Arden to create the “Alliance Fleet”. Now they are heavily tariffed on outgoing merchandise and rich imports, making them near-impossible to afford.
## Favors
The Caelish have a method of owing and trading in favors. In their culture, you always pay back a favor that someone does for you. Nothing is for free, especially not when you ask for help. If you aid someone in their time of need, they owe you one favor.

These favors can pile up, and can be collected whenever the person who is owed desired. Nowadays, the Caelish have evolved this tradition into something more official, and to collect a person “invokes” the favor.

## True Names
The Caelish place a much deeper, spiritual emphasis on True Names. They have rituals to pass them on, and take it seriously when one is revealed. True Names, to them, are not only chosen by someone else but have the power to *change* the person they’re chosen for.

They have a tradition for Naming Day, and a whole celebration for the child (reaching the age of 6), who receives their True Name from an authority in their lives, usually a parent. They hold that the age of adulthood is 20, a herald of the number 2 they hold dear. Usually, someone will receive a second True Name on their twentieth birthday, and the celebration is called Rebirth Day.
## Story Beads
They use beads to tell stories, sometimes woven into hair or curtains or rugs. This is how [[#the Relay]] works. Each color and material and engraving tells a different piece of the story.
## The Relay
There are a number of Relays that travel about Carlen, gathering information and exchanging it with other Caelish.

The beads system is misunderstood by the [[Arden]], and allows the Caelish to “encode” messages in seemingly mundane objects. Relays appear as “merchants” to the Arden, selling these objects to pass around information.
## Linguistics
Their names are inspired by Dutch/Netherlands and old English.

The Caelish have a fascination with the number two. Their language is made up of letter pairs, and everything they do is in twos. Everything should have a pair. They believe odd things bring bad luck, like odd-numbered names, odd-numbered shipments, etc.
