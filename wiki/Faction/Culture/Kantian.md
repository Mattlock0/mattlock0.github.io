The **Kantian** are a [[Culture]] in [[Northern Hearth]] which reside mostly in [[Kantian Triad]]. Most Kantian are [[Tebrene]].
## Government
Has been an autocracy, oligarchy, and even a confederation, but is currently a principality. Known for their various wars and stigma against outsiders. Very versed in sea-faring and ocean-based war machines. There’s always war in Kantia.
