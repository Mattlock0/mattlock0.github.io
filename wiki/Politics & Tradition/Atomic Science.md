They call atoms (the smallest possible unit of matter) piths. When explaining them, scientists use this analogy:
“Have you ever seen a brick house? Each individual brick is a small segment of a larger object, and when put all together with bonds they create a house. From far away, you cannot see the individual bricks, just the house.

You could take that same understanding and apply it to everything. All objects are made from piths, tiny bits of matter that, when bound together, make the things we see and interact with.”
