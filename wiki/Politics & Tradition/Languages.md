## Imperial
The current primary language of the Alliance of Nations. It was originally called Elden, but Eldenguard changed the name to reflect the feel of the Alliance. They still pressured the other regions in the Alliance to use it. Now, much of Tiereth and some of Baro-Sassan speak it.
## Tyrean
A language which still sees some use in Tiereth, primarily due to how easy it is to write. Those in Tiereth use it when writing letters or messages to each other. However, it is not an accepted language to the rest of Northern Hearth.
## Elliscript
An old, dead language which was spoken before the [[Age of Deceit]].
