**Slate** is a rock-like [[Material]] found in [[Northern Hearth]] that is Vis-inert.

A durable metamorphic rock that can last for several hundred years without maintenance. It is formed from a mixture of clay, shale, and volcanic ash. It is mostly grey, but can be purple, green, or cyan. It is also difficult to quarry due to its nature to split along the "grain" (veins of the stone).

For all these reasons, slate is the material which is entirely Vis-inert. It does not conduct, absorb, or even interact with Vis, and cannot be manipulated in any Spiritual way. (Via Veins, [[Willpower]], Fate, etc) It is unchanging.

It's good for writing on, was used as whetstone, is fireproof, and is difficult (if not impossible) to make.

Many cultures see it as a symbol of death (inert/created from ash) and use it for tombstones.
