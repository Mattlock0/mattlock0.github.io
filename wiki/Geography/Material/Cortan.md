**Cortan** is a crystalline [[Material]] found in [[Northern Hearth]].

A translucent green crystalline/enamel substance that grows on the peaks of the Kapechi Mountains. It's quite tough, but if it is crushed and mixed with water it creates a sticky glue-like substance that will harden when exposed to air.

If two objects are glued together with cortan, they count as one object in relation to Vein manipulation. Spiritually, it connects the two ideas together. The Tyrean use it in their wedding ceremonies.
