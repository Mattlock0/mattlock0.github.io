**Ferrite** is a [[Material]] found in [[Northern Hearth]].

A naturally occurring rock absorbs photons. It releases stored photons when force is applied to it. When it is empty, it absorbs ambient sources of light. The stronger the force that is exerted on it, the stronger the light it gives off at once. Found in abundance in Tiereth and Dreyynir.
