**Axial Wood** is a living [[Material]] found in [[Northern Hearth]] that is Vis-conductive.

It is difficult to cut, as the fibers are both tough and alive. Used to make Root Constructs, though only by using whole sprigs. Cutting Axial Wood destroys the fibers which allow Vis-conductivity.
