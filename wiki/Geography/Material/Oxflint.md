**Oxflint** is a crystalline [[Material]] found in [[Northern Hearth]].

A rock found almost exclusively in Dreyynir. It is shiny and black, like obsidian. And like obsidian, it is relatively fragile and can be shattered easily. But like flint, when a sharp, localized pressure is applied on it, oxflint produces heat. When it is shattered, oxflint can even explode, sending sharp splinters of rock every which way.
