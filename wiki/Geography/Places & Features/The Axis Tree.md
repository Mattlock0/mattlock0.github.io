**The Axis Tree** is a large, planet-sized tree whose branches pierce the clouds and entirely cover the northern hemisphere of [[Hearth]]. The Axis Tree rests at the northern pole of Hearth, and from it shoot massive Roots carrying [[Veins]] and other strange, divine powers.

Many religions consider the Axis Tree to be the root of all life, from which all were carved and to which all will return. It serves as a consistent “north” (though Hearthians call it Treeward), and is the source of the Root Barrier that encircles all of [[Northern Hearth]].
