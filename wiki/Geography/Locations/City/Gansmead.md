**Gansmead** is a [[City]] in [[Adraine]], within [[Northern Hearth]]. It is governed under [[The Arden Alliance]].
## Districts
### Harbourwall
The docks and central trade route.
### Pardish Square
There is a dais in the center used for events and announcements.
### The Western Exchange
The primary market district of Gansmead.
### The Twists
A group of long-standing buildings that formed the original Gansmead when it was owned and founded by the [[Caelish]].
### Renevat Reach
The rich, noble zone with high quality land and easy access to the beach. Overlooked by the [[Knights]], mostly maintained by personal guards.
### Ringside
The lower-income area of Gansmead, ruled over similar in fashion to Sonmore by the close Arden Borough.
### Arden Borough
While the repository is their outpost, the Borough is a ruling region consisting of Knights in a more permanent position. They guard the ringward gates and ensure nobles have easy access to the city.
### Treeside Gate
The northernmost gate leading to the Tree.
### Northwind Gate
An alternate, northeast gate commonly used by trading caravans to easily access the Western Exchange.
### High Alliance Repository
A gate and set of outposts used exclusively by the Knights.
