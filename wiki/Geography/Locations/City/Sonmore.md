Sonmore is the “capital city” of Carlen. It was built by the Alliance when the [[Caelish]] complained about not enough housing, but quickly after being built they realized it was more a prison than a paradise.

Number of buildings: 1976
Population: ~12000
## Districts
As Sonmore as fallen into disrepair, the undrough forced to remain there have taken to calling the distinct sections of it names.
### The Gates
There are four sections called by The Gates, and they are usually all patrolled by Knights. They mark the entry and exit points from Sonmore (official ones, anyways). Each gate has an Knight-given name, but they’re much sunnier than any resident of Sonmore would choose.
### The Stretch
All the main roads in Sonmore. Most undrough stay out of these (thus the name) because they’re mostly in clear sight of patrolling Knights. Most undrough will do their best to avoid the roads and route through the Stacks or, if they must, the Halls.
### The Stacks
The Stacks were part of the first wave of Alliance-built housing. They consisted of many low-rooved houses, and were quickly filled up. When Sonmore received even more residents, the Alliance scrambled to build more (see the Halls) and the first wave slowly disintegrated. The overabundance of population degraded the houses until they were abandoned, crumbling shells of what they once were.
### The Halls
The Halls are the second—and still functioning—wave of Alliance housing. They were built different from the Stacks; made with tall, several-story rooms, but these cost money. As such, many undrough that live here are the crime bosses or their highest goons. Many undrough can’t afford these and take up residence in the stacks, but the Halls are truly dangerous. Called the Halls because walking through the narrow roads between them is like crossing a long hallway.
### The Tops
The Tops were not built for undrough. The resident Knights of Sonmore had to live somewhere, and they couldn’t take the Stacks or the Halls. So the little greenery that was left in Sonmore was paved over to build the Tops: housing for the Knights and for important visitors. To be caught wandering through the Tops is a torture sentence. An undrough might be strung up in the central Stretch to be verbally and sometimes physically lambasted by Knights. Few undrough join in.
### The Deposit
Trading post for the Knights where tend to filter through the citizens of Sonmore.
### Gatebridge
A miniature Arden city for governors and Knights who could not fit into the Tops.
### The Old Road
A portion of Caelish Road that has been abandoned.
