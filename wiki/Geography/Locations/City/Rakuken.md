Rakuken is one of the capital cities of East Tiereth, and their pride and joy on the Windy Mountain. It is an isolationist city, and the home of many [[Breath Vein]] advancements like windmills and air conditioning. They do not have many Roots, but the few they do have they use to protect the perimeter of the town.

There is a high wall surrounding the peak of the mountain, and around the livable parts is Rakuken. The summit of the Windy Mountain extends higher into the sky, but the people below live on a flattened plateau just low enough to support life. A Root coils through the mountain, warming it and allowing the citizens to grow wheat in such high altitudes. (the summit is a little shorter than Pike’s Peak, so it’s around 12k feet)

The [[Tyrean]] of Rakuken have a unique cocktail of [[Breath Vein]] which allows them to ?. They view Veins as blessings from the heavens, though they only really have the one type. They believe their distant relatives, the [[Ancient|Ancients]] were angels, living above the cloud barrier and communing directly with God (who they believe is in the heavens at the top of the Tree). They do think they’re one of the closest human groups to the heavens, and therefore should not be tainted with outsiders.

Within Rakuken is their old religious sector, with shrines to the gods of heaven, and tree, and sun. They haven’t been torn down yet because they frankly haven’t needed the space, and there’s some sentiment attached to them. But no one really worships them anymore.
