A prison the Archria keep turned Valkrin in. They siphon the Vis from those in prison to power all their technology above ground. This Vis goes through a filtering system so it is not corrupted by the time it reached them. To keep their Vis reserves full, the government makes up laws to get Archria imprisoned and extend their sentences at a moment’s notice.
