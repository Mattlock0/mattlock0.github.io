**Tiereth** is a [[Continent]] in [[Northern Hearth]]. Within it are the [[Republic of Tiereth]] and the [[Aristocracy of Tiereth]].
## Geography
A mountainous, snowy forested continent home to the unique, air purifying [[Alester Pines]].

Much of Tiereth is used for domestication and animal farming, as there is rarely space and adequate conditions for crops. Some special areas, like Rakuken, flourish with both. The rest of their food, they gather from hunting.

There are lots of Tiereth cities are dedicated also to mining the insides of the mountains. The Kapechi range is rich with iron, copper, and the increasingly valuable Slate. Often, the center of the Kapechi mountains have large caves systems and ravines, making exploring them a worthwhile endeavor. Approximately a third of mountainous land is barren.

There are heated oases scattered across the Kapechi Range (with Roots running through them) that the Tyrean believe are blessed by the gods. They are lovingly called the Oases of Reflection. Many are perfectly still when they are found, as the pools do not offer good drinking water (but good water for feeding crops). The area around these oases is lush with green life.

## Notable Locations
### Eastern Province
- **Rakuken** (Holy Capital) — A city built around the peak of the Windy Mountain. Rakuken used to be the sole capital of Tiereth, but after a [[Valkrin]] attack and their religious delve into Connections, they isolated themselves to “purify” their Identities, having only a few sacrificial Tyreans man the Trading District, their sole form of communication with the outside world. They still send missives and trade goods with the rest of East Tiereth, but they leave governing to the second capital.
- **Kalidom** (Governmental Capital) — Where Rakuken is a capital in name and tradition, Kalidom is a capital in governance and commerce. A city only overshadowed by the cap of the Windy Mountain, Kalidom is situated on one of the flat-top plateaus of the Kapechi Range, just three mountains separate from Rakuken. They are primarily responsible for rulership over East Tiereth, and their Leydesh resides within Kalidom. The city is connected to others via gravel road and to Jeldain with a secret tunnel.
- **Jeldain** — A small mining city downwind from Kalidom, but on the same mountain.
