**Adraine** is a [[Continent]] in [[Northern Hearth]]. Within it are [[The Arden Alliance]] and the [[Timocracy of Threyda]].
## Geography
A largely flat continent with mountains in the south and rock plains in the north.
## Notable Locations
### Elden Region
- **[[Lothrame]]** — The capital of Eldenguard and home of both the Chambers and Exale, the [[Xander Fulgard|Lord Regent]]’s palace.
- **Morendar** — Highbrow city where most of the Houses reside. Some take up residence in Celentide, but those not directly connected to the Lord Regent have a home in Morendar
- **Resen** — The science/Savant capital of Eldenguard, very close to the capital.
- **Aporite** — Trade center of Eldenguard and the birthplace of railways.
- **Renevat** — Lynn mentioned leading Corporal Herides through it on one of their previous campaigns.
- **Alendria** — Birthplace of railways and a junction to other parts of Eldenguard.
- **Faridel** — Trade Center. Very close to Alendria so as to transport goods across Eldenguard.
- **Ladicia** — One of the few cities within Eldenguard in which Caelish and Elden live together in mostly harmony. Far enough from the capital that they aren’t pushed into their tradition or hierarchy, Ladicia boasts towering waterfalls and tall aspen trees.
### Carlen Region
- **[[Sonmore]]** — The Elden-constructed “capital” of Carlen that boasts “free” housing for Caelish.
- **Gansmead** — A port city in the far west of Carlen. Used to be the central hub of trade and commerce, but since under Knight rule it has simply become an extension of Aporite.
- **Terane** — A connection city between Gansmead and Sonmore. [[Knights]] are less common here.
- **Arhelm Peak** — A mountain missing its upper half where, in ancient times, a Flux Vein battled off against a [[Valkrin]] threatening Carlen. Neither of them survived the explosion.
- **Forebrook** — Destroyed; the result of a valkrin attack. Used to be the home of the Freya family.
### Intica Region
- **Wirral** (Capital)
- **Tomb of the Fallen Hero** — The resting place of Thalen, the victor of the Ascension War. It is nearby Kelos, Thalen’s birthplace.
### Threyda Region
- **Darundor** (Capital)
- **Anatha** — A deep-seeded, underground city dedicated to collecting works on the Ideals. The heart of Anatha is a multi-ringed library.
