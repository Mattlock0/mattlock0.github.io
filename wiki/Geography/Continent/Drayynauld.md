**Drayynauld** is a [[Continent]] in [[Northern Hearth]].
## Geography
A mountainous, cave-filled continent with beaches in the western peninsula (often called [[Marion Reach]]) and large valleys in the east. A land of mountains, canyons, and a volcanic region in the northeastern quadrant.
