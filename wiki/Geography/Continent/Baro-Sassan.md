**Baro-Sassan** is a [[Continent]] in [[Northern Hearth]].
## Geography
Kingdom of sand. Baro-Sassan edges on the Root Barrier, allowing its residents to see the night sky in its fullest.
## Notable Locations
- **Sabaria** (Capital)
- **Taendra Village**
- **White Serpent River** — Runs through Baro-Sassan and gives off an eerie mist in the morning. Said to hold supernatural healing properties.
## Resources
