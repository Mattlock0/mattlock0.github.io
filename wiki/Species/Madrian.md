**Madrian** are a [[Species]] of [[Northern Hearth]]. They originated from [[Riddem Luther|Riddem]] and [[Leyn Orsev|Leyn]] and make up the [[Entic]] and [[Tyrean]] cultures.
## Physical Features
Madrian have dark skin, thick eyebrows, and light-colored eyes, usually green. They have sharp, hardened spikes on their elbows (made from keratin) and long, deep nailbeds that can carve into [[Axial Wood]].

Madrian are more connected to [[The Axis Trees]] than most, and as such are reliant on photosynthesis as well as food and rest to create energy. If they are away from the sun for too long, they become lethargic and can fall unconscious easily.

Most Madrian have thin skin which allow some to breathe *through* it. This makes them excellent Breath Veins, but hydration is a much more important factor for them, becoming deadly to them much faster than for others. Their blood is iron–rich, which allows them to regulate oxygen better but gives their blood a dark tint.

A strain of red hair runs through the Madrian race in East Tiereth.
## Spiritual Features
Madrian are born with a Nature of [[Conviction]] and [[Wrath]] and an affinity for [[State Veins]] and [[The Heart]].

Their affinity allows them to connect to [[The Axis Trees]] through the [[Roots]] and feel the “paths of the world”, which are shadows of the [[World Line]] and the ruts that plants, animals, and weather fall into. This makes them perfect hunters. Madrian can consume a piece of [[Axial Wood]] and have it to grow in their stomachs to allow them to always feel these paths.

Successive children born to Madrian have different strengths chosen at seemingly random by a Madrian’s genes. This leads to an often birthright-related hierarchy within families, where each sibling is given a different blessing.
